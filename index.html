“Jehovah detests a devious person, but His close friendship is with the upright.”
— Proverbs 3:32
We can learn about the importance of having a sincere heart from Jesus’ first meeting with Nathanael. Although Jesus had never met Nathanael, Jesus said: “See, truly an Israelite in whom there is no deceit.” Jesus admired Nathanael’s sincerity and commended him for it. Psalm 15 shows that pleasing Jehovah includes treating others well and avoiding speech that harms them.
